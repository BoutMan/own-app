Single-arm row

Back · core · 2 × 10 per arm

How to do it

Step 1StartOne knee and one hand on a chair or bench, back flat and parallel to the floor. Load in the free hand, arm straight.
Step 2MovePull the load toward your hip, elbow close to your body. The torso doesn't rotate. Lower over 2 seconds.
Watch outCommon mistakeTwisting the torso to pull heavier. If your shoulders don't stay parallel to the floor, the load is too heavy.
